Start and end shows from the top of the Look up screen. Every buy and sale in between is tagged with the show, and ending a show saves a list of them.

Reading cards from photos (optional)

Card photos are always saved without this. If you add a key, the camera button becomes “Scan card with camera” and also reads the set code, number and language from a photo for you. Reading sends the card photo to Claude, which reads the set code, number and language. It needs a Claude API key from console.anthropic.com. API use is billed there per photo, separately from any Claude subscription, and costs about a cent or less per scan. Set a monthly spending limit in the Console to be safe.

Japanese card prices

Japanese prices come from Cardmarket (Europe) in euros, through TCGdex, and are converted to dollars. They run lower than US prices for many cards, so treat them as a floor and use the eBay button to check.

Sample cards for testing

Adds about 20 real Japanese, Chinese and English cards with made-up buys and sales from three past shows, so the stock, log and reports have something in them. Sample entries are marked and can be removed without touching real ones.

Backup

Records are saved on this device, and online too when sync is on. The spreadsheet has every buy and sale, your stock and your shows, for Excel or Google Sheets. The backup is for restoring the app on another phone.
This deletes every card and every buy and sale on this device, and on every synced device too if sync is on. It can't be undone unless you have a backup.
Card data and prices: TCGdex (tcgdex.dev). English prices are TCGplayer market prices. Chinese cards have no price source, so you set the price yourself.
